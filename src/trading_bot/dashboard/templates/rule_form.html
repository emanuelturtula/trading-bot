How a rule is written

A rule fires on a closed candle when its conditions hold, and is checked on every ticker of its timeframe it is assigned to.

Fields
name
Unique; shown in signals.
signal
BUY or SELL.
timeframe
{{ timeframes | join(', ') }}. It cannot change while the rule is assigned to tickers.
cooldown_bars
Candles to stay quiet after a signal (0 for none).
conditions
{"all": [...]} (every condition holds) or {"any": [...]} (at least one holds).
Conditions and grouping

Each condition is {"left": operand, "op": operator, "right": operand}. Operators: {% for operator in operators %}{{ operator }}{{ ', ' if not loop.last }}{% endfor %}. The crossings compare the last two candles.

A group may contain conditions and groups of the other kind, one level deep: an any inside an all, or an all inside an any. For example, "RSI below 30 and (close above SMA 200 or MACD above its signal)":

"conditions": {"all": [
  {"left": {"indicator": "rsi"}, "op": "<", "right": {"value": 30}},
  {"any": [
    {"left": {"price": "close"}, "op": ">",
     "right": {"indicator": "sma", "params": {"length": 200}}},
    {"left": {"indicator": "macd", "output": "macd"}, "op": ">",
     "right": {"indicator": "macd", "output": "signal"}}
  ]}
]}
Operands
{"price": "close"}
A column of the candle: {{ prices | join(', ') }}. "volume" is read the same way, even though it is not a price.
{"value": 30}
A fixed number.
{"indicator": "rsi", "params": {"length": 14}, "output": "value"}
An indicator below; params and output fall back to the defaults.
